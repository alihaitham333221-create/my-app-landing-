Purpose of the Application

EduTrack is an educational management platform designed to help educators, instructors, and students manage coursework, monitor academic performance, track student progress, and organize learning metrics efficiently.

By integrating with Google OAuth, EduTrack provides users with secure sign-in to access their personalized academic dashboard and track educational milestones.

Privacy Policy

EduTrack respects your data privacy. We only request permissions necessary to authenticate your identity via Google Sign-In. We do not store, sell, or share your personal account information with third parties. All accessed user data is used strictly to provide educational tracking features within active user sessions.
